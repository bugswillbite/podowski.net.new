All Around Sound

I created a website for the Rochester-based Dj and entertainment company All Around Sound. The client requested a functinal booking website with interactive elements, leaning towards a frutiger aero aesthetic - while still staying professional.

Role

Developer & Designer

Tools

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Figma, Wix

allaroundsound.net

All Around Sound is a DJ company based in Rochester, NY. They have been a part of the community for nearly 30 years, but recently came under new ownership. The new owner wanted to modernize their website, and requested a professional platform leaning into the frutiger aero aesthetic, including unique responsive elements that reflect DJ equipment.

A primary focus of the project was creating a streamlined, interactive booking platform that directly connects customers with custom DJ packages, equipment add-ons, and scheduling.

Translating the client's request for a Frutiger Aero aesthetic, I designed glassy UI cards, subtle gradient glosses, and tactile interactive elements inspired by DJ mixers and soundboards.

Special attention was given to maintaining high visual polish while keeping the user flow clear, intuitive, and conversion-focused for potential clients inquiring about weddings, corporate events, and private parties.

To elevate the brand's 30 year legacy under new leadership, I created interactive UI/UX components that bridge physical sound culture with modern web functionality. Key work included building a custom JavaScript pricing plan calculator, allowing clients to dynamically build event packages and estimate costs in real time based on time, equipment needs, and add on services. I also designed tactile, interactive elements, such as a digital vinyl record crate component for browsing frequently asked questions.

Building long term trust for a legendary entertainment brand required a frictionless way to capture and showcase client satisfaction. I implemented a custom 'Leave a Review' submission workflow designed to transform post event feedback into automated, dynamic social proof.

The system leverages the Wix CMS backend to securely capture structured submissions, including client names and detailed testimonials, straight from a clean, responsive frontend form interface.

By connecting this input pipeline directly to dynamic datasets, new reviews automatically feed and populate across designated testimonial modules on the live site. This eliminates manual content updates, keeps social proof fresh and authentic, and highlights genuine community experiences for prospective event planners browsing the platform.

The updated platform successfully bridged legacy community trust with a bold, modern web presence that sets All Around Sound apart from conventional event services. By combining playful Frutiger Aero aesthetic details with a detailed booking workflow, the project delivered an interactive, memorable experience that boosted direct booking inquiries and positioned the new ownership for long-term growth.
